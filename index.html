A Legacy of Hospitality & Celebration

Situated on Sarangapani Street in close proximity to the landmark temples of Kumbakonam, Moorthy Kalai Arangam is a premier, centrally air-conditioned kalyana mandapam engineered to host seamless, memorable functions.

Whether hosting a traditional grand wedding with 2,000+ floating guests, a compact family function in our mini hall, or booking comfortable lodging for visiting relatives across our 22 AC rooms, our dedicated team and round-the-clock power backup ensure complete peace of mind.
